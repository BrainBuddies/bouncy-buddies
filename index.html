Welcome to Bouncy Buddies

Your personalised buddy who sits with you while you do the boring stuff, and cheers you on when you finish each step.

  • Body doubling in your pocketYour buddy keeps you company while you work, so starting and carrying on feel easier.
  • Cheering you on, one step at a timeYour buddy helps you break up your jobs into achievable steps.
  • Self-care modeYour buddy can remind you to drink water, have a snack, eat lunch, stretch and breathe. The reminders come when you finish a step or task, never in the middle of focus. You choose what to switch on.
How it works
  1. Design your own buddy: shape, colours and the way it cheers you on.
  2. Add the tasks you have been putting off, and give yourself a time for each one.
  3. Every finished task earns a gold star. Spend your stars in the Buddy shop to treat your buddy: a milkshake, a crown, a car or a rocket ship.
  4. Start the race against the clock. Your buddy stays with you, changing colour as time passes. Tap your buddy when you finish each step, it cheers you on, and then helps you set up the next step.
  5. Finish early to bank free time and earn medals and trophies. A trophy means you've banked 20 minutes to treat yourself, guilt-free. You did the work first, so the rest is yours.

Made by Brain Buddies® in Bristol, for bouncy brains. Nothing to sign up for. Your buddy and tasks stay on your own device.
